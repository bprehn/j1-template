Stay Safe, Stay on Groomed Trails!

At Mount Kato, your safety is our top priority. To ensure everyone has a fun and secure experience on the slopes, we strictly prohibit skiing and snowboarding outside of groomed areas. This policy is a crucial part of our safety program.

Here's why this policy is in place:

Unforeseen Hazards:

Ungroomed areas hide dangers like irregular terrain (deep snow, hidden rocks, stumps, uneven ground), obstacles (fallen trees, branches), and poor visibility, all of which can lead to falls and serious injuries.

The Dangers of Following Tracks:

Don't follow tracks into ungroomed areas; they may be from experts. This can lead to situations beyond your skill. Off-piste, you risk losing control on unpredictable snow, encountering cliffs or dense trees, and becoming disoriented without markers. Stay safe by skiing within your abilities on designated trails.

Rescue Challenges:

If an accident occurs outside of groomed areas, reaching you quickly can be significantly more challenging for our ski patrol. The time it takes to locate and assist an injured individual is greatly increased in ungroomed terrain, which can have serious consequences.

Impact on Natural Environment:

Skiing and snowboarding off-piste can damage fragile plant life and disturb wildlife habitats. Our commitment to responsible recreation includes protecting the natural beauty of Mount Kato for everyone to enjoy.

By staying on groomed trails, you're not only protecting yourself but also contributing to the safety and well-being of our entire ski community and the preservation of our natural environment. We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to seeing you safely enjoy Mount Kato's slopes!
