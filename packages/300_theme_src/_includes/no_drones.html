Unauthorized Drone Use is Prohibited at Mount Kato

At Mount Kato, we are committed to providing a safe, enjoyable, and private experience for all our guests. For these crucial reasons, the unauthorized operation or use of unmanned aerial systems (UAS), commonly known as drones, is strictly prohibited on or above Mount Kato property.

Here's why this policy is in place:

Safety of Guests and Staff:

Collision Risk: Inexperienced drone operation can lead to malfunctions, crashes, injuring skiers/staff, and damaging property like chairlifts or snowmaking equipment.
Interference with Operations: Drones can disrupt essential resort activities such as ski patrol, rescue efforts, or helicopter operations (less common at Mount Kato, but a general ski resort concern).
Distraction: Drones can distract guests, potentially causing accidents or near-misses on slopes.

Privacy Concerns:

Drones equipped with cameras can capture images and videos of guests and staff without their consent. This raises significant privacy concerns and can make individuals feel uncomfortable or observed while enjoying their time on the mountain. Mount Kato is dedicated to protecting the privacy of everyone on our property.

Regulatory and Legal Compliance:

FAA Regulations: All drone operations are subject to Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) regulations. Operating a drone over people or property without proper authorization can lead to serious legal consequences for the operator and potentially for the resort.
Resort Jurisdiction: As private property, Mount Kato has the right to control activities on its premises, including drone use. Our policy ensures a safe and orderly environment for everyone.

Potential for Nuisance and Disturbance:

Noise Pollution: Drones can be noisy, disrupting the peaceful mountain experience that many guests seek.
Wildlife Disturbance: Drones can disturb wildlife, especially in natural areas adjacent to our groomed trails.
