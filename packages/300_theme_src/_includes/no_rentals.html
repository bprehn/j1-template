No Rental Equipment in Terrain Parks Please

At Mount Kato, the safety of our guests and the longevity of our equipment are top priorities. Therefore, we have a strict policy regarding the use of rental equipment within our Terrain Parks. Rental equipment is NOT permitted in any Mount Kato Terrain Park.

We appreciate your cooperation in helping us maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for all Mount Kato visitors. If you are interested in exploring freestyle skiing or snowboarding, we encourage you to consider purchasing your own equipment designed for Terrain Park use and to seek instruction from our certified instructors.

Here's why this policy is in place:

Equipment Design and Durability:

Our rental skis and snowboards are designed for general recreational use and groomed trails. They are not built to withstand the stresses and impacts associated with Terrain Park features such as jumps, rails, and boxes. Using rental equipment in the park can lead to significant damage to the equipment, rendering it unusable for future renters and incurring costly repairs.

Safety of Beginners:

Terrain Parks present unique challenges and require a higher level of skill and experience. Many individuals using rental equipment are beginners who are still developing their fundamental skiing and snowboarding techniques. Allowing inexperienced individuals into Terrain Parks significantly increases the risk of accidents and injuries, both to themselves and to others in the park.

Incident Reduction:

By restricting rental equipment from Terrain Parks, we aim to greatly reduce the number of incidents involving beginner skiers and snowboarders who might inadvertently venture into or attempt features beyond their current ability level.

Visual Identification:

To help reinforce this policy, all Mount Kato rental skis and snowboards are clearly marked with "No Rentals In Parks" stickers. We ask all renters to respect these markings and understand that their rental equipment is not to be used within the designated Terrain Park areas.
