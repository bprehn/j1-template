Renting Equipment

After getting your rental form at the ticket windows you can make your way to the rental shop where our friendly staff will help you get fitted with the proper equipment for your age size & ability.

The Rental shop is located just east of the Chalet, two buildings down from The Guest Services office. Enter the rental bulding at the first door on the southwest corner of the rental building (the side facing the hill). There are counters available to fill out your rental forms. There is also a scale and ruler available to help those that don’t know their height or weight.

The rental staff will help fit your helmets and boots as well as adjust your skies and snowboards.
